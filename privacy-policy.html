Privacy Policy

Shopify WhatsApp Integration · Effective date: May 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CareNetSolutions ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you install or use the Shopify WhatsApp Integration application (the "App").

1. Who we are

The App is provided by CareNetSolutions. The App adds a floating WhatsApp chat button to a merchant's Shopify storefront and provides an admin dashboard to configure that button.

This policy applies to merchants who install the App on their Shopify store. It does not create a direct relationship between CareNetSolutions and a merchant's customers who visit the storefront.

2. Information we collect

We collect only the information needed to operate, secure, and support the App.

2.1 Merchant and shop information

When you install or use the App, we may process:

  • Your Shopify store domain (for example, example.myshopify.com)
  • OAuth session and authentication data required to connect the App to your store
  • Shopify API access tokens associated with your installation
  • Basic Shopify staff account details provided through Shopify (such as name, email, and user ID), if available
  • App permissions (API scopes) granted during installation

2.2 App settings you provide

When you configure the App, we store the settings you enter, including:

  • WhatsApp country code and phone number
  • Default WhatsApp message
  • Button label (accessibility text)
  • Whether the WhatsApp button is enabled or hidden

These settings are stored as app installation metafields in your Shopify store so the storefront button can display the correct information.

2.3 Technical and operational data

We may automatically collect limited technical information such as:

  • Server logs (request timestamps, IP address, and error logs)
  • Webhook events related to app installation, uninstallation, or scope updates

3. Information we do not collect

The App is designed to minimize data collection. We do not:

  • Collect or store personal information about your storefront customers
  • Read, store, or monitor WhatsApp conversations or message content
  • Collect customer order, payment, or checkout data through this App
  • Track customers across websites for advertising purposes
  • Send automated WhatsApp messages on your behalf

4. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • Install, authenticate, and operate the App
  • Save and display your WhatsApp button settings on your storefront
  • Maintain app security and prevent misuse
  • Respond to support requests and troubleshoot issues
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms
  • Improve the reliability and performance of the App

5. Legal basis for processing (EEA/UK merchants)

If you are in the European Economic Area or United Kingdom, we process data on these bases:

  • Contract: to provide the App and features you request
  • Legitimate interests: to secure, maintain, and improve the App
  • Legal obligation: where required by applicable law

6. How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Shopify: to install, host, and operate the embedded app
  • Hosting providers: to run the App and store session data
  • Meta / WhatsApp: only indirectly when a visitor clicks the WhatsApp button and is redirected to WhatsApp — we do not transmit customer data to Meta through that click
  • Legal recipients: if required by law, court order, or to protect rights and safety

7. Storefront visitors and WhatsApp

The App displays a WhatsApp link on your storefront using the phone number and message you configure. When a visitor clicks the button, they leave your store and open WhatsApp (operated by Meta).

Meta's terms and privacy policy apply to WhatsApp use. You, as the merchant, are responsible for compliance with applicable laws and Meta's policies.

8. Data retention

  • Session data: retained while the App is installed and as needed for security
  • App settings: stored in Shopify metafields until changed or the App is uninstalled
  • After uninstall: we delete associated shop session data from our database

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures including HTTPS, access controls, and secure storage of session credentials. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

10. International data transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including where our hosting providers operate. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards.

11. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of your personal information. Contact us below to exercise these rights. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

12. Merchant responsibilities

  • Provide a WhatsApp number you own or are authorized to use
  • Ensure your storefront privacy notices describe how you communicate with customers
  • Comply with applicable privacy, marketing, and consumer laws
  • Comply with WhatsApp and Meta platform policies

13. Children's privacy

The App is intended for businesses and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will revise the effective date at the top of this page. Material changes may be communicated through the App or by other reasonable means.
